# Privacy Policy — Blocks: Puzzle Game

**Effective date:** October 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Blocks: Puzzle Game** (the "App"), developed by **Tufrengi** ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App on Android.

The App is a single-player puzzle game. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no forms, and no servers operated by us. The App itself does not send your game data to us.

The App includes the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** (Google AdMob) to show advertising. Google, not Tufrengi, handles the information the SDK uses. This policy keeps the two clearly apart:

- **Information handled by the game itself** (Section 2), which stays on your device.
- **Information handled by Google AdMob** (Section 3), which is subject to Google's own policies.

## 2. Information stored locally by the App

To remember your progress and preferences, the App stores the following on your device only:

- your **best score**;
- your **audio settings** (master volume and sound-effects volume);
- your **haptics setting** (vibration on or off).

This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is used only inside the App, for example to show your best score and to apply your settings.

Your current game in progress, your score during a game and in-game items are not saved between sessions.

Android may include app data, such as these settings, in your device's own backup if you have device backup turned on. Your device's backup settings control this, not the App.

The App does not intentionally collect your precise location, contacts, photos or other files, microphone or camera input, or any content you create.

## 3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show a banner advertisement at the bottom of the game screen.

When ads are requested and shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information from your device, such as:

- the **advertising ID** of your device;
- **device and network information** used to request, deliver and measure ads (for example device type, operating system and the network connection);
- information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped.

This processing is carried out by Google. Depending on your consent choices (see Section 4), Google may use this information to show personalised or non-personalised ads, to measure ad performance, and to prevent fraud.

For details on what Google collects and how Google uses it, please see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

During development and testing, the App may use Google's test ads. When live advertising is enabled, ads are delivered through Google AdMob using the consent mechanism described in this policy.

## 4. Consent and privacy choices

The App uses **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to manage consent for advertising.

- Each time the App starts, it checks with Google whether consent is required for you, for example if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.
- Where consent is required, Google's consent form is shown, and you can choose whether to consent.
- The App requests ads only when the consent status allows ads to be requested.
- Your choices are stored on your device by Google's consent SDK.

You can review or change your choices at any time in the App: **Settings → Privacy → Privacy Choices**. This option appears where Google indicates it is required.

## 5. How information is used

- **Information stored locally by the App** (Section 2) is used only to run the game: to show your best score and to apply your audio and haptics preferences.
- **Information processed by Google AdMob** (Section 3) is used by Google to provide, personalise (where you have consented), measure and protect advertising, as described in Google's policies.

Tufrengi does not use the information described in this policy to build profiles about you or sell your personal information.

## 6. Data sharing and third-party services

The App does not send your locally stored game data to us or to any third party.

The only third-party service in the App is **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK, including Google's User Messaging Platform). When ads are requested, the SDK communicates directly with Google, and Google may share information with its advertising partners as described in its policies and as permitted by your consent choices.

The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services of our own.

## 7. Data retention

- **Locally stored game data** stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings.
- **Information processed by Google** is retained by Google according to Google's own policies.
- **Consent choices** are stored on your device by Google's consent SDK until you change them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

## 8. Children's privacy

The App is a general puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself stores only the gameplay preferences listed in Section 2, on the device, and does not send them to us.

Advertising in the App is provided by Google AdMob and is subject to Google's policies. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us (Section 12) and we will help where we can.

## 9. Security

The App stores its own data only on your device and does not send it to us, so there is no Tufrengi server holding your game data. Information processed for advertising is handled by Google under its own security practices.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the App's handling of information to the minimum it needs to work.

## 10. Your privacy choices

You can:

- **Change your advertising consent** at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Privacy Choices**, where this option is shown.
- **Reset or limit advertising personalisation** using your device's settings, for example by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings.
- **Delete the App's locally stored data** by clearing the App's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.
- **Contact us** with any privacy question (Section 12).

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when the App's features or its advertising configuration change. The updated policy will be published at the same location with a new effective date. Please review it from time to time.
